# Fey
An animated dektop pet that lives on top of your dektop. Fey walks arround your taskbar, sleeps, chases your cursor and eats fishes you can give her.

## Features
- Transparent, always on top, click through window. 
- State machine that controls the differents states it can have.
- Drag and drop + gravity: pick Fey up, drag it arround and move it wherever you want that she will always return to your taskbar.
- Sleep: falls asleep after a long period of inactivity, interact with her and she will wake up.
- Chase: randomly follows your cursor.
- Fish: spawn a fish by pressing "f" and Fey will run to eat it.
- Sound: randomized meow (pitch and volume) when clicked.
- Dynamic light, color and intesity follows your computer´s clock.
- Persistance: saves its position and starts asleep on launch.
- Hover name tag: when you hover her it will appear a name tag displaying "Fey".
